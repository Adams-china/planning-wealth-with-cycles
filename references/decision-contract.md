# Decision contract

Produce the final answer in this order.

## 1. Verdict

One sentence with exactly one action: buy, do not buy, wait, hold, reduce, or rebalance.

## 2. Amount and execution

- amount now;
- maximum target weight;
- number and conditions of tranches;
- review or rebalance trigger.

Use currency amounts when the user supplied a budget. Do not give percentages alone.

## 3. Evidence table

| Layer | Evidence | Effect | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Household safety | ... | positive/neutral/negative | 0.0–1.0 |
| Macro/cycle | ... | ... | ... |
| Valuation | ... | ... | ... |
| Quality | ... | ... | ... |
| Execution fit | ... | ... | ... |

Label each material statement as book thesis, current fact, or inference when confusion is possible.

## 4. Countercase and invalidation

- strongest reason the recommendation could be wrong;
- observable condition that changes the action;
- worst plausible loss or drawdown range when evidence supports one.

## 5. Confidence and caveat

State overall confidence, weakest link, data timestamp, and the main caveat. Below 0.80, explicitly shrink or defer the position.

For household allocation, show safety, growth, and hedge accounts; weights and amounts must sum exactly. For a single stock, require business quality, balance sheet, earnings, valuation, and concentration review; macro/cycle cannot exceed 20%. For banks include asset quality, net interest margin, provisions/capital, deposit franchise, profitability, valuation, and dividend sustainability. For gold, distinguish portfolio insurance from momentum chasing and forbid leverage for beginners.
